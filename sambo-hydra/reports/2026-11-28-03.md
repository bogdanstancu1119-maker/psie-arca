## Attacker origins: what we publish, and why

We keep one visible part and hide the rest, like 203.0.***.***, so the report can be read and checked without exposing a person or a network. **Technically.** The address is masked at ingestion: the first two IPv4 octets or the first two IPv6 groups stay visible, the rest becomes ***.
