## Attacker origins: what we publish, and why

**In plain words.** When someone attacks Hydra, we never publish their full address. We keep one visible part and hide the rest, like 203.0.***.***, so the report can be read and checked without exposing a person or a network. We do not answer the attack, we do not touch the attacker's machine, and we do not scan it.
**Technically.** The address is masked at ingestion: the first two IPv4 octets or the first two IPv6 groups stay visible, the rest becomes ***. Only an HMAC-SHA-256 fingerprint with a server-side salt is stored, so repeat attempts are recognised without keeping the address. Each repeat increments a counter.
